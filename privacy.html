bw-server-backup — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

bw-server-backup is a private, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner, Bruce Wang, to copy encrypted backups of his own server to his own Google Drive.

  • Access requested: Google Drive, limited to files the app itself creates (drive.file). It cannot see or change any other files.
  • Data: the app uploads encrypted backup archives (encrypted before upload with restic) and reads them back for restore. It does not collect, sell, share or analyse any personal data, and it has no other users.
  • Storage: the OAuth token is kept only on the owner's server and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: bruceyangwang1989@gmail.com
